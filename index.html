FINDING — Green/Amber data, a valid peer set or hard external rate, and a driver identified. Sized, ranked, roadmapped.
LEAD — the signal is real but the evidence is thin (no peer cluster, fitted parameter, or missing file). Becomes an investigation task, never a committed number.
CONTEXT — directional or non-comparable. Displayed for orientation, excluded from every total.
